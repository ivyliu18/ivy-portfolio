Case 01 — NESS Wellness

Making "find clients for a brand" into a system anyone can execute

A Taiwan agency took on NESS's corporate wellness promotion and needed to build a B2B client acquisition mechanism from zero. The challenge: most executors had no sales background, yet needed to efficiently identify enterprise contacts and generate genuine interest.

"How do you make someone who doesn't know sales get a decision-maker to say 'tell me more'?"
01
Find the Right Person

Defined 6 target roles (HR, L&D, ESG, Admin, Department Heads, Medical/Education), each with its own prioritisation logic and outreach sequence.

02
Generate Interest, Not a Pitch

Designed 9 lead-source strategies (LinkedIn, job boards, company websites, FB communities, trade associations, events). Built separate low-pressure message templates for HR, Admin, and Manager personas.

03
Clear Handoff Trigger Points

Listed all "interested" signals. Once met, immediately hand off — no chasing, no progress tracking. Task complete at handoff.

Core Design Principles
  • Don't mention the brand first — confirm the need
  • Never quote pricing or describe courses
  • Clear stop point: handoff = done
  • Reduce executor decision cost to near zero
9 Lead Sources
  • LinkedIn (foreign companies)
  • 104.com.tw (local SMEs)
  • Company website HR mailbox
  • Facebook HR communities
  • Trade association member lists
  • Existing client industry expansion (highest success rate)

"Data measures execution results. This document shows the system that makes results possible."

Case 03 — Apollo (MayoHR)

Strong product. Foreign enterprises aren't buying. The problem is messaging, not the product.

Apollo's HR system has clear advantages in Taiwan regulatory compliance and full-stack integration — theoretically perfect for foreign companies in Taiwan. But LinkedIn content had almost no engagement. They were speaking the language of local Taiwanese HR, not foreign decision-makers.

"A foreign HR Manager doesn't want a feature list. They want: what KPIs can this system help me show to HQ?"
Diagnosis
The Positioning-Marketing Gap

Apollo has everything to penetrate foreign enterprises (ISO27001, automatic local law updates, full-module integration), but messaging targets local SMEs — missing foreign HR/Finance's need-language entirely.

Entry
Three Levels of the Foreign Enterprise Decision Chain

Global HR/IT sets standards → Regional HR/IT screens solutions → Local HR uses daily. Strategy: reach local HR first, equip them with English materials to pitch to HQ.

Content
Content Restructuring Direction

Bilingual white paper on Taiwan HR regulatory pain points for foreign firms. KPI-framed case titles: "Payroll processing time cut 40%, avoided significant labour inspection fines".

Apollo's Entry Advantages
  • Auto-updates for Taiwan Labour Standards Act
  • ISO27001 — passes foreign enterprise IT audits
  • Full-module single platform integration
Root Causes of Low Foreign Penetration
  • LinkedIn content audience = local HR only
  • No English content or foreign company case studies
  • Missing the KPI language of foreign decision-makers

"Win local HR first, then give them English materials to advocate upward to HQ."

How I Work — Thinking Framework

Three consistent frameworks
behind every project.

Whether it's fintech operations or brand strategy, I enter every chaotic situation with the same thinking logic.

01
Ask "why not" first
Before designing a process, I define the boundaries — what this person shouldn't be doing often clarifies what they should. Defining exclusions reduces execution friction and raises success rates more than any instruction list.
02
Decompose fuzzy requests into dimensions
A brief like "help the brand find clients" gets decomposed into: who's the target (role) × where to find them (source) × what to say (message) × when to stop (handoff trigger). Once decomposed, anyone can execute.
03
Design testable hypotheses
Every strategy should be verifiable. I embed KPIs, A/B test logic, or milestone checkpoints at design time — so whoever comes after me can track and adjust, even when I'm not in the room.
